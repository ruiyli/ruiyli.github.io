I am currently working as an Applied Research Engineer at Tencent PCG, where I focus on developing ranking models and exploring the OneModel approach for QQ's search engine. Prior to this, I was a Master's student at the [Key Laboratory of Network and System Security (NSS Lab)](https://nss.xidian.edu.cn/), [Xidian University](https://www.xidian.edu.cn/), under the supervision of Associate Professor [Guangxia Li](https://faculty.xidian.edu.cn/LGX4/zh_CN/index/386575/list/index.htm). During my academic journey, I had the privilege of collaborating with distinguished researchers such as Principal Researcher [Peilin Zhao (formerly at Tencent AI Lab until 2025, now a professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University)](https://peilinzhao.github.io/), Assistant Professor [Zhiqiang Xu (MBZUAI)](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=0R20iBMAAAAJ&hl=en), and Dr. [Xiao Lu (Ericsson)](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=6J6Xy8IAAAAJ). 

My research interests lie in generative search/recommendation systems, large language models (LLMs), multimodal learning, and diffusion models. I am actively exploring these areas and have also conducted research on ADMM-based optimization for multi-task learning and addressing graph data heterogeneity in federated learning. 

<span style="color:red"> I am still open to PhD opportunities and am eager to continue my research. I also welcome collaborative research with others, so please feel free to reach out if you're interested in working together. </span>
