I am currently working as an Applied Research Engineer at Tencent, where my work centers on generative search and ranking: I build and iterate the ranking-stage CTR prediction models behind QQ Search, and explore OneModel, a unified generative modeling approach to search. Prior to this, I was a Master's student at the [Key Laboratory of Network and System Security (NSS Lab)](https://nss.xidian.edu.cn/), [Xidian University](https://www.xidian.edu.cn/), under the supervision of Associate Professor [Guangxia Li](https://faculty.xidian.edu.cn/LGX4/zh_CN/index/386575/list/index.htm). During my master's studies, I also collaborated closely with Principal Researcher [Peilin Zhao](https://peilinzhao.github.io/) (at Tencent AI Lab until 2025, now a professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University), Assistant Professor [Zhiqiang Xu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=0R20iBMAAAAJ&hl=en) at MBZUAI, and Dr. [Xiao Lu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=6J6Xy8IAAAAJ) at Ericsson.

My research interests lie in (multimodal) large language models, generative search and recommendation, and diffusion and flow-based generative models. Earlier in my research, I also worked on ADMM-based optimization for multi-task learning and on data heterogeneity in federated graph learning.

<span style="color:red"> I am keenly interested in frontier research along these directions — (multimodal) LLMs, generative search and recommendation, and diffusion/flow models. If you are working on similar problems, I would love to hear from you, so please feel free to reach out to discuss or collaborate. </span>
